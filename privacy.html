Privacy policy for Howzat

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version

Howzat is a free cricket arcade game with no developer-operated game backend, ads or tracking. Your career scorebook and settings stay on your device. Apple's optional Game Center handles authentication, leaderboards and achievements; an already signed-in account can submit scores and achievement progress automatically.

Starting with version 1.7, the app sends anonymous usage summaries, such as how long you play and which screens you open, to help improve the game. They contain no name, account or exact scores, and you can turn them off in Settings.

What's stored on your device

Howzat saves the following locally using iOS's standard UserDefaults storage:

  • Your best innings, measured by runs, and the strike rate from that game. A full best-innings scorecard is retained when available, even after it leaves the recent list.
  • Lifetime statistics: games played, total runs and total time played.
  • Detailed career totals for fully recorded completed innings: innings count, cumulative runs, balls and 1/2/4/6-run shot counts.
  • Per-bowler balls, runs, dismissals and shot outcomes for recorded innings. First-bounce length is grouped into 24 bands along the game's pitch; deliveries without a recorded bounce are counted separately. Cumulative bowler totals remain available after older scorecards leave the recent list.
  • Up to 20 recent completed innings, with a local record identifier, completion date, runs, balls, strike rate, active duration, dismissal reason and shot counts. Scores displays the latest 10. Detailed career totals continue after older entries leave this list.
  • This week's best innings (runs and strike rate). A new week starts every Monday at 00:00 UTC.
  • Achievement progress: which of the 12 achievements you have earned, your century count and how many weeks you have scored in. Kit colours and bat stickers unlock from these on-device achievements.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, bat size, footwork mode, Left/Right control side, player appearance, kit colour and bat sticker.
  • Whether you completed or skipped the first-play pad lesson.
  • Optional tips: verified App Store transaction identifiers and whether each tip is counted or refunded, so a tip is not counted twice. Tips unlock no content or features.
  • Review timing: the latest completed-play date and consecutive-day count, plus the two most recent review-request dates and last requested app version. These records limit how often the app asks for a review.
  • Usage analytics (version 1.7 and later), unless you turn them off: a random install identifier, the date analytics first started on this device, and at most 64 summaries waiting to be sent.

Earlier installs keep their known lifetime games, runs and best-innings snapshot. Historical balls, dates, shot counts and bowling detail that were not saved are not reconstructed. The scorebook labels partial history rather than treating missing records as zeros. Abandoned innings are not added to bowler history.

The local scorebook and settings are not uploaded. There is no iCloud sync for these records. Game Center maintains a separate leaderboard score when you use that service. Anonymous usage summaries contain only rough ranges, as described under Analytics.

Game Center

Howzat uses Apple's Game Center for optional global leaderboards and achievements:

  • Best innings (howzat.best): all-time personal-best runs. Positive completed innings and your saved local best can be submitted.
  • This week (howzat.best.weekly): your best runs in the current week, restarting every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Only the current week's best is submitted.
  • Total runs (howzat.career.runs) and innings played (howzat.career.innings): your lifetime runs and number of finished innings, submitted after each innings and when you sign in.
  • Career average (howzat.career.average): submitted after you have 20 recorded innings, replacing your previous entry. Versions before 1.6 also submitted a career strike rate (howzat.career.strikerate); 1.6 and later no longer do.
  • 12 achievements (howzat.ach.*): earned achievements and progress toward the career ones are reported after recorded innings and when you sign in.

Apple handles your Game Center identity.

The app checks Game Center authentication at launch without presenting a sign-in sheet. The trophy always opens Scores, where Career, Bowlers and Recent work offline. Sign-in and Apple's full leaderboard require explicit actions in Scores or Settings. The game is fully playable without Game Center.

Opening Scores requests a bounded leaderboard refresh when authenticated. The app loads at most the top 100 entries of the selected leaderboard, including Game Center player identifiers, display names, ranks and scores, plus Apple's total ranked-player count and your separate rank and submitted best. Worldwide displays up to 10 loaded entries. These rankings are held in memory and are not added to your saved career history.

Apart from the scores and achievement progress listed above, the app does not upload innings scorecards, bowler analysis or other career totals. It has no all-player innings database. A summary of loaded personal-best scores describes only that leaderboard sample, not worldwide innings statistics.

Game Center is Apple's service. Apple's handling of your player identity is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Network access

Howzat has no separate game backend. Game Center can use the network for authentication at launch, score and achievement submission after authentication or a completed game, the bounded Scores refresh and the full leaderboard interface. Apple handles that traffic through GameKit. Apple also handles optional tip purchases and reviews through its services. Unless turned off, usage summaries go to TelemetryDeck when the app opens, returns to the foreground or moves to the background.

Cricket recordings are bundled in the app and play offline.

Sharing a scorecard

After an innings, Share creates an image of that scorecard on your device and opens Apple's share sheet. The image shows only that innings' runs, balls, strike rate, dismissal, shot counts and any new-best badge. Howzat sends it nowhere itself; it goes only where you choose to share it.

Optional tips

Settings offers optional one-time tips through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment and holds your purchase history under its own privacy policy. Howzat has no purchase server and never sees your payment details.

The app records only the verified transaction identifier and its counted or refunded state on your device, and shows a thank-you count from that record. Tipping changes nothing in the game.

Optional App Store reviews

After five consecutive days with a completed innings and an innings of at least 50 runs, Howzat may request Apple's review sheet when you explicitly return to Main Menu. The request waits two seconds and is cancelled if you start playing, open a sheet or leave the foreground.

The timing records stay on your device. Requests are limited to two in a rolling 365 days, at least 120 days apart, and require a newer app version than the last request.

Apple decides whether to show the sheet and handles any review you submit. Howzat does not receive your rating or learn whether you submitted one.

Analytics

Starting with version 1.7, Howzat sends anonymous usage summaries to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to learn which parts of the game people use. Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage turns this off at any time. Turning it off deletes the install identifier and anything not yet sent. Earlier versions send no analytics.

What is sent:

  • Rough session length and active playing time, in ranges such as "3-10 minutes".
  • Counts from that session: innings played, how they ended, and whether you opened Settings, Scores sections, look lists, the tip jar or the share sheet.
  • Your best runs that session and in your career, only as ranges such as "25-49".
  • Your current game settings and chosen looks, which settings you change, and whether the pad lesson was completed or skipped.
  • Lifetime milestones such as your 10th innings, with rough days since you first opened the app.
  • App version, iPhone model and iOS version.

Each summary carries a random identifier created on this device and hashed before sending. It is not your name, Apple Account, Game Center identity, advertising identifier or vendor identifier. Nothing is linked to your identity, and nothing is used for tracking or advertising. Exact scores, scorecards, bowler analysis, location and contacts are never sent.

Howzat includes no crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. Apple separately provides aggregate App Store and crash statistics from devices whose owners chose to share them with developers.

Third-party services

TelemetryDeck receives the anonymous usage summaries above; its privacy policy covers how it stores them. Howzat includes no third-party SDKs or libraries: it sends the summaries with Apple's own networking. No Howzat account or sign-up is required; optional Game Center sign-in and optional tips are handled by Apple.

Children's privacy

Howzat is a general-audience game. It has no advertising or tracking and does not require a Howzat account. It collects no personal information from anyone; usage summaries are anonymous and can be turned off. The local records, analytics and optional Apple services described in this policy apply to players of any age.

Deleting your data

Deleting Howzat removes the app's local data from your device, including its scorebook, statistics, weekly best, achievement progress, earned looks, settings, tip-count records and review-timing records.

Turning off Share anonymous usage, or deleting the app, removes the analytics identifier and any unsent summaries. Summaries already sent are anonymous and cannot be traced back to you, so they cannot be looked up or deleted for a particular person.

Apple manages Game Center records, including leaderboard scores and achievements, and tip purchase history separately. Deleting Howzat does not delete those Apple-managed records. Use Apple's controls or contact Apple for help with those records.

GitHub support issues are public. Share only information you are comfortable making public.
