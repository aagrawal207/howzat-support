Howzat

A cricket arcade game for iPhone. Pull the bat with the trackpad, defend the wicket, and hit the numbered boundary rails.

Play with your phone held sideways. Score 1, 2, 4 or 6 runs from the rails, and keep the ball and bat away from the stumps. Your score is your runs.

Howzat is a free, general-audience game for iPhone running iOS 17 or later. It has no ads or analytics tracking. Gameplay and your local scorebook work offline; Game Center rankings and tips are optional Apple services.

Help and privacy

  • Player support: how to play, controls, sound, scores and reporting a problem.
  • Privacy policy: local records, optional Apple services and deleting your data.
