Howzat

A cricket arcade game for iPhone and iPad. Pull the bat with the trackpad, defend the wicket, and hit the numbered boundary rails.

Play with your iPhone held sideways, or with your iPad in any orientation or window size. Score 1, 2, 4 or 6 runs from the rails, and keep the ball and bat away from the stumps. Your score is your runs.

From version 1.13, the game is styled like a county-ground match programme and scorebook; in Dark Mode, Settings, Scores and Your player switch to a darker "evening edition". Each innings closes on a scorebook page you can share, and at the start of each week, players signed in to Game Center who batted the week before may see last week's top 10.

Howzat is a free, general-audience game for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. It has no ads or tracking; from version 1.7 it sends anonymous usage summaries that you can turn off in Settings. Gameplay and your local scorebook work offline. iCloud sync, Game Center rankings and tips are optional Apple services.

Help and privacy

  • Player support: how to play, controls, Settings, Your player, resuming an innings, iCloud, sound, scores, last week's top 10, sending feedback and reporting a problem.
  • Privacy policy: local records, the iCloud copy of your progress, optional Apple services, anonymous usage summaries, the feedback email and deleting your data.
